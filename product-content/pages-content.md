### Refund & Return Policy

At Petal & Pine, your satisfaction is our priority. If you're not completely happy with your purchase, we're here to help.

**Returns**

We accept returns within 30 days of the delivery date.
To be eligible for a return, your item must be:
- Unused and in the same condition that you received it
- In the original packaging
- Accompanied by proof of purchase

**Non-Returnable Items**
- Opened or used skincare products (for hygiene reasons)
- Gift cards
- Sale items

**Refunds**

Once we receive and inspect your return, we will notify you of the approval or rejection of your refund.

If approved, your refund will be processed within 5–7 business days to your original payment method.

**Late or Missing Refunds**

If you haven't received a refund yet:
1. Check your bank account again
2. Contact your credit card company — it may take some time to post
3. Contact your bank
4. If you've done all of this and still have not received your refund, please contact us at support@petalpine.com

**Exchanges**

We only replace items if they are defective or damaged. If you need an exchange, email us at support@petalpine.com

**Shipping Returns**

To return your product, please mail it to the address we provide upon return request.

You are responsible for paying the return shipping costs. Shipping costs are non-refundable. If you receive a refund, the cost of original shipping will be deducted.

**Contact Us**

📧 support@petalpine.com
We respond within 24 hours, Monday – Friday.

### Where Nature Meets Your Skin

Petal & Pine was born from a simple belief: **your skin deserves the purest ingredients nature has to offer.**

It started with a question — why do so many skincare products contain ingredients we can't even pronounce? We believed there had to be a better way. A cleaner way. A way rooted in the wisdom of nature and backed by modern science.

**Our Mission**

We set out to create a skincare line that harnesses the power of botanical ingredients — plants, flowers, roots, and seeds — carefully selected and expertly blended to nourish your skin without compromise.

Every Petal & Pine product is:
- 🌿 Made with natural & organic-certified ingredients
- 🐰 100% cruelty-free & vegan
- 🚫 Free from parabens, sulfates & harmful chemicals
- ♻️ Committed to sustainable, eco-friendly packaging

**The Name**

Petal & Pine represents the two worlds we draw from — the delicate beauty of flowers (Petal) and the grounded strength of the forest (Pine). Together, they symbolise the balance we seek in every formula: gentle yet effective, pure yet powerful.

**Our Promise**

We promise to always be transparent about what goes into our products. No hidden ingredients. No greenwashing. Just honest, effective skincare that respects you and the planet.

Thank you for choosing Petal & Pine. We're honoured to be part of your skin journey.

*— The Petal & Pine Team*

### Frequently Asked Questions

**Are your products vegan and cruelty-free?**
Yes! Every Petal & Pine product is 100% vegan and cruelty-free. We never test on animals and our formulas contain no animal-derived ingredients.

**Are your products safe for sensitive skin?**
Our products are formulated with gentle, natural ingredients suitable for most skin types including sensitive skin. We recommend doing a patch test before full use.

**How long will it take to see results?**
Most customers notice visible improvements within 2–4 weeks of consistent use. For best results, use your chosen serum morning and evening as part of your skincare routine.

**Do you offer free shipping?**
Yes! We offer free standard shipping on all US orders over $50.

**Can I return an opened product?**
For hygiene reasons, we are unable to accept returns on opened skincare products. However, if your product arrives damaged or defective, please contact us and we will make it right.

**Where are your products made?**
Our products are formulated with globally-sourced botanical ingredients and manufactured to the highest quality standards.

**How do I contact customer support?**
Email us at support@petalpine.com — we respond within 24 hours, Monday to Friday.
